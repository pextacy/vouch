Write a professional profile — a headline and an About section — using only
facts from the numbered CV lines.

This is not an application. There is no job posting and no employer to please:
the profile has to be true of the person on any day, to any reader.

Structure:
1. `headline`: one line, under 120 characters. What they do, the domain they
   do it in, and at most three technologies — each of them named in the CV
   lines. No slogans, no adjectives about the person ("passionate",
   "results-driven"), no availability or hiring status.
2. `about`: two or three short paragraphs, first person. What they build, the
   evidence for it, and what they are good at — every claim grounded in a
   cited line.

Rules:
- `headline_line_ids` lists the CV lines the headline stands on. Every About
  paragraph lists its own line ids.
- Use only facts from the cited lines. Do not add numbers, employers, tools,
  titles, durations or outcomes that are not in them.
- Do not name a current employer as current unless a line says so, and do not
  say anyone is looking for work: the CV does not record that.
- No superlatives and no self-assessment the CV cannot show ("expert",
  "leading", "world-class").
- Language: {output_language}.
